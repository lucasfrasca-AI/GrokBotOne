Thanks to Harry Godber and the Spark Foundation for hosting this year's Spark Festival and for the UTS Startups warm-up talks that kicked the week off. Some takeaways include notably the illusion of a strong Australian ecosystem on the index, despite an inherent dialogue / funding-clarity problem between startups and government.

An interesting point was raised on how the European Union's heavier regulation has slowed growth and potentially hindered development at the cost of safety and security. Leading into the upcoming Australian Privacy Act 1988 changes in December 2026, compliance and enforcement could stall innovation and expansion of AI startups, products and implementation. The same rule applies in the public sector for agentic AI: trust, accountability and workforce capability have to be in place before systems that touch real services roll out.
